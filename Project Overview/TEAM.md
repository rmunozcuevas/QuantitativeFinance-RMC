# Team and decision ownership

**Raymundo is the sole day-to-day lead.** He owns scope, priorities, assignments, deadlines, onboarding, student support, equipment access, workshop coordination, technical decisions, and final acceptance of project deliverables.

The cohort contains five accepted student contributors. This plan does not invent their identities or assume whether Raymundo occupies one of those five places. If he is in the cohort, he takes one contributor responsibility below; otherwise the five students share them under his coordination. There is no sixth contributor assignment.

| Responsibility | Concrete output | Initial owner | Peer check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Data preparation | Documented dataset and cleaning notebook | Student slot 1; assign September 23 | Slot 2 |
| Finance calculations | Calculator exercise, cash-flow assumptions, sensitivity table | Student slot 2; assign September 23 | Slot 3 |
| Baseline and model evaluation | Baseline, chronological evaluation, comparison and limitations | Student slot 3; assign September 23 | Slot 4 |
| Hardware and reproducibility | Jetson setup guide and repeated runtime measurements | Student slot 4; assign September 23 | Slot 5 |
| Learning materials and demonstration | Lesson packet, decision brief, presentation integration | Student slot 5; assign September 23 | Slot 1 |

These are primary responsibilities, not five isolated projects. Each student explains their work to another student, contributes to the shared demonstration, and rotates into a second area after the first milestone. Integrating the brief is shared work; slot 5 does not write everyone else's results.

## Support and accountability

Raymundo supplies a scoped task, success criteria, reviewer, target date, and access to needed equipment. Students report blockers at the next check-in or sooner when another task depends on them. Raymundo records the resolution or revised plan on the task board.

Peer review checks reproducibility and explanation. Raymundo accepts final deliverables. For his own technical work, another student checks the evidence before he records acceptance. If Raymundo is unavailable, students continue already scoped work and record questions; there is no automatic substitute lead.

Summer retains periodic funder reporting based on reviewed evidence. Sanchin and Cail have no standing operational assignment or approval requirement in this alternate plan. Any future advisory task must be explicitly recorded by Raymundo; historical authorship remains intact.
