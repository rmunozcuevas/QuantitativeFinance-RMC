# Start here

## What you are joining

Five accepted students will build one shared example that connects a financial calculation, a baseline forecast, a model comparison, and a demonstration on the Jetson. Raymundo runs the project and is the person to contact for assignments, blockers, equipment, reviews, and scheduling. Summer handles periodic funder summaries.

## First week: September 23–29, 2026

1. Tell Raymundo your availability, interests, and setup needs through the team's existing private channel. Do not post contact details or applications here.
2. Read the [overview](Project%20Overview/README.md) and the handbook's scope and data rules.
3. Get a task and peer reviewer from the [task board](Project%20Overview/TASKS.md). Names and dates are assigned Wednesday, September 23, 2026, not assumed here.
4. Complete one small exercise: reproduce a calculator result, rerun a baseline, or test the setup guide.
5. Put a short dated entry in your contributor folder and link the output. Use the full template for experiments and hardware setup.
6. Ask your peer to check the work, then send it to Raymundo for completion review.

## Weekly routine

Kickoff and role assignments are Wednesday, September 23, 2026. Meeting time is to be confirmed by Raymundo in Eastern Time. Starting September 30, proposed Wednesday check-ins last 30 minutes, plus one shared 60–90-minute work session when availability permits. Report what finished, what is next, and anything blocked. Each student should have one active deliverable at a time.

At the check-in, Raymundo sets priorities, assigns or adjusts owners and deadlines, resolves blockers, and reserves equipment. Peer reviewers help check work; they do not become additional project leads.

## Where files go

- Personal reasoning, attempts, and results: `Lab Notebooks/<name>/`.
- Shared notebooks and scripts: `shared/analysis/`.
- Lessons, exercises, and facilitator notes: `shared/workshops/`.
- Setup and benchmark procedures: `shared/protocols/`.
- Dataset descriptions and acquisition steps: `shared/datasets/`.
- Reviewed outputs and final brief: `shared/deliverables/`.

A finished task has a linked output, enough instructions to repeat it, a note about limitations, a peer check, and Raymundo's final review. Failures and blocked work are valid updates; do not claim they are completed results.

See the [project calendar](Project%20Overview/CALENDAR.md) for the September 23 kickoff and role assignments, followed by proposed milestone dates.
